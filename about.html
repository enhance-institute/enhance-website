About ENHANCE

The Independent Structural
Audit Function for Climate Finance

ENHANCE Institute examines whether climate-finance systems, projects, instruments, and claims are causally coherent, financially aligned, evidentially sufficient, and structurally capable of achieving their stated purposes.

Foundational Documents

Institutional Basis

ENHANCE's mandate, independence principles, and institutional position are established in three public documents, available in full below.

Founding Statement · July 2026
Who We Are
ENHANCE was developed to examine why systems created to address climate risk can produce outcomes different from those they declare. Finance and technology are necessary to protect climate systems. They are not sufficient unless the projects they support are causally credible, financially aligned, and capable of producing verifiable outcomes.

A project may be well intentioned and still be structurally unable to achieve what it promises. A financial instrument may mobilize capital and still be mismatched with the country, beneficiary, or risk it is meant to serve.

ENHANCE was built to examine these gaps.

Declaration · July 2026
Independence
ENHANCE defines climate-finance structural audit as an independent, evidence-based examination of whether the causal, financial, institutional, beneficiary, and measurement structures of a climate-finance activity are aligned with its stated purpose and capable of supporting its claimed outcomes.

Structural audit is not a statutory audit, financial-statement audit, regulatory certification, legal opinion, credit rating, or assurance engagement. It is not an official evaluation performed under a mandate from any assessed institution.

ENHANCE's independence is based on transparent rules. Findings are governed by evidence and method — not by the preferences of assessed parties, sponsors, clients, political groups, or advocacy interests.

Official Position Statement · July 2026
Position
ENHANCE Institute supports effective, accessible, and accountable climate finance. We do not advocate for the weakening, delegitimization, or dissolution of institutions created to mobilize and deliver climate finance.

Structural audit asks whether that finance is capable of achieving its own stated purpose. An audit is not punishment. It is a disciplined examination of evidence, design, implementation conditions, and results.

ENHANCE rejects the selective use of its findings to imply that all climate finance is ineffective or unnecessary. A structural finding applies within the scope, evidence, and limitations stated in the relevant report. Those who agree with an ENHANCE report do not acquire ownership of it.

Institutional Identity

Three Integrated Layers

ENHANCE operates across three integrated functions, with structural audit as the primary and public-facing mandate.

Primary
Independent Structural Diagnostic Authority.
Public audit reports, published without client relationship, examining climate-finance structures against stated purposes.
Secondary
Structural Audit Body.
Private structural evaluation engagements, subject to independence and conflict-of-interest review prior to acceptance.
Tertiary
Climate System Design Organisation.
Framework development and methodological architecture supporting the diagnostic and audit functions.
