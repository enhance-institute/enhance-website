About the ISAR Series

Independent Structural Audit Reports

The ISAR series constitutes ENHANCE's primary public audit output. Each report is produced without a client relationship. ENHANCE selects subjects independently, applies defined analytical frameworks to publicly disclosed documentation, and publishes findings without prior review by assessed institutions.

Evidence Base
All findings derive from publicly available documents. ENHANCE does not rely on non-public information or internal disclosures from assessed institutions.
Independence
No client relationship exists for public audits. ENHANCE discloses material conflicts of interest and does not accept analytical direction from assessed parties.
Determination Framework
Structural findings are expressed as: Supported · Conditional · Unverified · Structurally Void. No finding attributes intentional misconduct.
