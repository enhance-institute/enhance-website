The Audit Sequence

QUANTIFY · VERIFY · UNDENIABLE

QUANTIFY
To make a claim structurally visible.
ENHANCE decomposes climate-finance claims into their underlying architecture: problem definition, causal pathway, intervention logic, financing structure, delivery environment, monitoring boundary, outcome claim, and impact assertion. A claim that remains narrative cannot be audited. A claim must first be made visible.
VERIFY
To test whether the structure can support the claim.
ENHANCE does not accept a claim because it is well-written, institutionally endorsed, policy-aligned, or supported by formal disclosure. Verification asks: does the intervention reach the stated problem? Does the theory of change close? Does the claimed outcome depend on unstated assumptions? Verification is the point at which narrative must submit to structure.
UNDENIABLE
Truth that cannot be dismissed by rhetoric or reputation.
Undeniable does not mean infallible. It means that once a claim has been quantified and verified, the remaining structural finding cannot be dismissed merely by rhetoric, reputation, or institutional preference. A structural gap becomes visible. A causal failure point becomes identifiable. Truth becomes undeniable when structure is made visible.
