Scope & Boundary

What Structural Audit Is — and Is Not

Structural audit connects questions that are often distributed across separate documents, teams, and stages of the project lifecycle.

What Structural Audit Examines
  • Causal integrity of the theory of change
  • Financial instrument alignment with country conditions
  • Beneficiary reach and distribution pathways
  • Private capital activation assumptions
  • MRV systems and performance evidence
  • Climate claims and mitigation baselines
  • Institutional arrangements and conflict-of-interest structure
What Structural Audit Is Not
  • Not a statutory audit or financial-statement audit
  • Not a legal opinion
  • Not an official evaluation by any assessed institution
  • Not a credit rating or investment recommendation
  • Not an assurance engagement under a recognised standard
  • Not a determination of intent or misconduct
